How the 0–100 score works

Every recorded document and court case is matched to an HCAD parcel. A parcel's score is the strongest signal in each group, plus a bonus for each additional group stacked on the same property. Two filings about the same death (say a probate case and an heirship affidavit) count once.

SignalGroupPoints
  • Stacking: +5 for each extra signal group on the same parcel (max +15).
  • Owner named deceased in a heirship affidavit, probate, or tax suit: +10.
  • No homestead exemption (likely absentee or rental): +5.
  • Delinquent taxes: +5 if $5k+ prior-year due, +10 if $15k+.
  • Aging: signals older than 1 year count 60%, older than 3 years 40%. Open tax suits and current delinquency never age.

Match confidence. High = the lot/block on the filing agrees with HCAD. Medium = the owner's name found exactly one parcel. Lower-confidence matches are never scored — they show in New filings → Needs review with the candidate parcels.

Sources: Hidalgo County Clerk official records, Hidalgo County Odyssey court records, Hidalgo CAD, and the county tax office. Unofficial copies; verify before acting on any single record.
